# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The tierstore community is for people to work on a storage system together. We expect everyone in issues, pull requests,
reviews and any other project space to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- give and take technical criticism about the work, not the person;
- welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.

These are not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal or political attacks, publishing someone's private
information, sexualised language or attention, and any conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and ban a person from the project for
behaviour that breaks this code. They will treat reports as confidential and will not retaliate against the person who
reported.

To report a problem, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/komira-ai/tierstore/security/advisories/new)
and say that it is a conduct report, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports about a maintainer go to
another maintainer.
